# Privacy Policy – Sidekick Factory

Sidekick Factory is a private tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (Sidekick Daily and Sidekick Diario).

- It requests permission to upload videos to YouTube on the owner's behalf (YouTube Data API).
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person.
- Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to upload videos approved by the owner.
- The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
